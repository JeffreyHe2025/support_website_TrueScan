About TrueScan

TrueScan is an on‑device tool that helps you spot synthetic, AI‑generated, or cloned speech. Record or import a short audio clip and TrueScan estimates the likelihood that the voice was produced by a machine rather than a human.

TrueScan provides an estimate to assist your own judgment. No detector is perfect — results should not be treated as definitive proof of authenticity, and should not be the sole basis for any legal, financial, or safety decision.

How to Use

  1. Add audio. Record directly in the app or import an existing recording.
  2. Scan. Tap Analyze and let TrueScan process the clip (best with a few seconds of clear speech).
  3. Read the result. TrueScan shows whether the voice is likely real or machine‑generated, along with a confidence indicator.
  4. Use your judgment. Treat the result as one signal among others, not a final verdict.

TrueScan provides a probability estimate, not a guarantee. Accuracy depends on audio quality, length, and the type of voice synthesis used. No. TrueScan is a helper tool. Do not use it as the only basis for legal, financial, employment, or safety decisions.
